# Bobyemi Limited Platform

## Overview

Bobyemi Limited Platform is a modern digital operations system designed to help Bobyemi Limited manage and optimize its daily business activities.

The platform is being built to digitize core business operations that are currently managed through notebooks, WhatsApp communication, memory, and manual records.

The system will support:
- Financial transaction management
- Electronics repair management
- CCTV installation and maintenance workflow
- Staff activity tracking
- Customer notifications
- Business analytics and reporting

The application is intended to serve as both:
1. A public-facing business website
2. An internal operations dashboard for staff and management
